SAF-GNO: Certified drift verification for agentic configuration of steel-building product families using a graph neural operator with statically admissible force fields

Tadesse G. Wakjira1,  Hana L. Goshu2
1Kennesaw State University 2The Hong Kong Polytechnic University

Abstract

Configuration agents need verification tools whose classifications are correct for every proposed building, whereas standard learned surrogates provide no deterministic error bound. SAF-GNO generates a displacement space and exactly equilibrated member force fields for each steel frame. A few Krylov iterations refine both fields inside a Prager–Synge enclosure, and every certified drift decision is correct for any value of the model parameters. A learned self-stress space and a learned equilibration operator, trained through the certified refinement, reduce the iterations required for certification.

Method

M1. Learned self-stress space. Member force modes decoded in member coordinates and projected exactly onto the null space of the equilibrium operator, used as dual Galerkin space and deflation space.

M2. Learned equilibration operator. A correction linear in the loads, in loop coordinates of the frame, added to the lift of the force method, and every particular solution satisfies equilibrium.

Trunk. Graph neural operator of AGNO generating the displacement space.

Certified refinement. Deflated conjugate gradients with algebraic multigrid on both sides of the enclosure, shared by every compared method.

Training. Trunk and both modules trained jointly through the refinement with a smooth form of the decision rule.
